# Keep a learner's Progress after an Approved Refund

When a Refund is Approved the learner loses access to the course (their
enrollment is removed), but their Progress — lesson completions, watch
history, quiz attempts — is kept indefinitely and simply becomes
unreachable. We considered deleting it, or purging it after a grace period.
Keeping it means a re-purchase restores Progress with no extra code, Refund
Eligibility on a later purchase still counts the earlier watch time and
completions (which closes a buy, watch, refund, re-buy loop), a mistaken
approval stays recoverable, and no scheduler is needed. Comments and ratings
are not Progress and are untouched either way.
